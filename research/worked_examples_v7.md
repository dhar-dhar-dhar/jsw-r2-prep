- Costs: processing and bank charges passed through; fraud and chargebacks 0.02 to 0.1 percent of value (A); risk, compliance, engineering. Net take rate for Indian payment aggregators 0.1 to 0.3 percent (A).

### F3 Insurance. Unit: a Rs 20,000 individual health policy sold online
- Distributor revenue: first-year commission 15 to 20 percent (A), Rs 3,000 to 4,000; renewals 5 to 10 percent (A); at 80 percent persistency, Rs 800 to 1,600 in year two (D).
- Live risk: IRDAI consultation 23 Sep 2026 proposing caps on commissions and expenses; PB Fintech down 43 percent in four sessions, Turtlemint lower circuit (S: Business Standard, 29 Sep 2026; Inc42). PB Fintech Q1 FY27 PAT Rs 163 cr (S: Entrackr).
- Distributor costs: marketing and agent payouts 40 to 60 percent of first-year commission (A), servicing, technology. Margin thin in year one, positive on renewals.
- Carrier: premium minus claims minus expenses. Digit Q1 FY27 PAT down 38 percent, premium down 8 percent (S: Entrackr); Acko FY26 profit Rs 43.6 cr, DRHP planned (S: Inc42). Individual health premiums up 29.7 percent Oct to Mar FY26 after GST went to zero on 22 Sep 2025 (S: Lok Sabha reply via A2Z Taxcorp). 100 percent FDI from 5 Feb 2026 (S: PIB).
- Claims software: Rs 50 to 300 per claim (A) against insurer cost Rs 500 to 2,000 per claim (A); gross margin 60 to 80 percent at scale (A).
- Must be true: distributor renewal revenue above 50 percent within three years and an uncapped commission; software price per claim below the insurer's own cost with measured loss-ratio gain.
